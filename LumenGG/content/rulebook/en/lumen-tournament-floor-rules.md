# Lumen Condenser Tournament Floor Rules

> **Reference date for original text display:** 2026 year 5 month 27 day
> **Original file name:** `Lumen Condenser Floor rule 20260530 revision.pdf`
> **Document nature:** An edited version of the official floor rules that has been reorganized to make it easier to search and reference on the web

## What is the floor rule?

Floor rules are common rules established to ensure that all players can enjoy the game fairly and enjoyably in competitions and events.

> Ready, fight in a good mood with fair and clean play!

> [! IMPORTANT]
> If there is any question about the rules or effect handling during the game, the player can ask the judge to make a decision. If both players disagree with the decision, they can ask the other judge to make the decision, but the judge has the power to decide whether to accept the request or not and make the final decision.

> [! NOTE]
> The anchors and table of contents numbers in this web compilation are editorial elements for navigation. There is no condolence number in the original PDF.

- Specific applications and final decisions are the responsibility of the judges or competition organizers.

# 1. Manners and participant roles



We participate in competitions and events with respect and consideration for others.

<a id="floor-player"></a>
## 1.1 player

Players must:

- This is presented so that the opponent can clearly see which card is ready during the game.
- When processing effects, be sure to recognize exactly which effect is being processed and how.
- Arrange the list so that it is clearly visible to the other person.
- Treat your opponent's belongings, including cards, playmats, tokens, and auxiliary tools, with respect.
- During the game, if you need to confirm Public Information, excluding your hand, you will do so after obtaining the opponent's permission.
  - Example: Check card for Break Zone
  - Example: Get Phase or check the card you take with the effect
  - Example: Battle Zone Check the exact effect of the card
- You must not do anything that may make the other person feel uncomfortable.
  - Threats made in a loud voice
  - Words and actions that can cause discomfort through self-talk
  - Play that intentionally takes time
  - Use of profanity
- When leaving your seat during a game, obtain permission from the opposing player, judge or tournament organizer.
- If you have any doubts about the rules or effects, you can ask the judge to make a decision.

### Re-request for decision to stop

- Players must follow the judge's judgment.
- If both players disagree with the decision, they can ask the other judge to make the decision.
- The judge has the power to decide whether or not to accept the request and make the final decision.

<a id="floor-spectator"></a>
## 1.2 spectator

- Spectators must observe good manners to avoid interfering with the operation of games and competitions.
- Failure to comply may result in a ban or expulsion from spectators at the discretion of the referee or competition organizer.
- Spectators cannot directly intervene in judging the rules of a game in progress or ask the judge about the rules. The actual player should contact the judge for any relevant situation.

<a id="floor-judge"></a>
## 1.3 Judge (referee)

- Judges follow the rules and cooperate with participants to ensure fair and smooth operation of the competition.
- We respond in a gentlemanly manner to set an example for all players.
- If we discover a player's lack of knowledge of the rules, cheating, or any behavior that could be considered a nuisance or interfere with the operation of the tournament, we have the right to intervene and point out or correct the action.
- If we discover incorrect handling of rules or effects, we can intervene in the game to request correct handling and impose a penalty depending on the content without any separate inquiry.

# 2. Cards and Auxiliary Tools



<a id="floor-legal-cards"></a>
## 2.1 Cards that can be used

The following cards are available:

- Lumen Condenser card developed by Yaoni Comic and distributed by Daewon Media
- A card with the same mark as Character Emblem of the character being used
- Neutral Emblem card
- Cards whose use is not officially prohibited
- Self-produced tokens that do not directly affect the game

When using self-made tokens, you must inform the other party exactly what token or state it represents.

<a id="floor-illegal-cards"></a>
## 2.2 card that cannot be used

The following cards cannot be used:

- pirated cards
- A card developed by Yaoni Comic and never distributed by Daewon Media.
- Cards not officially released
- Cards prohibited from use by the official

<a id="floor-deck-change"></a>
## 2.3 Deck formation and card changes during the competition

- Cards in the deck cannot be changed during the competition.
- Only if a card is damaged and cannot be used or its effects cannot be read, it can be replaced with a normal card.

> [! NOTE]
> The original text provides for an exception only for replacement of identical cards for damaged or unreadable cards. We do not provide procedures for changing decks for other reasons.

<a id="floor-marking"></a>
## 2.4 marking

Marking refers to any method that allows you to distinguish which card it is without looking at its face.

Marking of cards or protectors is not permitted under any circumstances.

### Marking example

- If you can tell them apart by scribbling on the back of the card
- If you can distinguish them by cutting or shaving the corners of the cards
- If stickers or other attachments are attached to the card
- In cases where wax, etc. can be applied and differentiated by feel

### autograph card

- If the card is identified on the back or has different specifications from the officially distributed card, it cannot be used.
- Signed cards are not recommended for use in official competitions or events, and the player is responsible for any disadvantages resulting from their use.
- If there is a signature on Technique Card other than Character Card and Trait Card, regardless of whether it is on the front or back, it is considered a marking and its use is prohibited.

<a id="floor-supplies"></a>
## 2.5 Auxiliary tools (supplements)

### unofficial aids

Except for the Lumen Condenser card for deck construction, you can basically use items not released by Yaoni Comics or Daewon Media.

### protector

- Specific cards should not be distinguishable.
- You must use a material that does not reflect objects clearly due to light reflection.
- All Technique Card in the deck except Character Card and Trait Card must use the same protector.
- If a judge or competition determines that a card is identified in its face-down state when using a transparent sleeve, disadvantages such as immediate replacement, loss of the game or competition, or disqualification may occur.
- We recommend using a sleeve that hides the back of the card whenever possible.

### Auxiliary tools that cannot be used

The following auxiliary tools cannot be used.

- Printed images that go against public morals, such as those designated as adult material under domestic law.
- Printed images that cause discomfort in others
- Images printed with malicious intent, such as insulting the deceased or hating a specific group
- Items with attachments such as stickers attached

At the discretion of the referee or competition, disadvantages such as immediate replacement or disqualification from the game or competition may occur.

# 3. Before the game starts



## 3.1 First Priority decision

The initial Priority holder is determined by random means agreed upon by both players, such as rock-paper-scissors, dice, or coin toss.

If conflict arises over how to decide, it is decided by rock-paper-scissors under the supervision of a judge.

## 3.2 Check before starting the game

Before starting the game, check the following:

- **Check character/characteristic/ultimate**
Place your characters, traits, and ultimate cards face up so they can be accurately recognized.
- **Check number of decks**
Check the number of decks you have and inform your opponent of the exact number.
- **OK Side Deck**
Turn the cards in your deck, excluding list 9 and starting card 5, into Side Deck and place them in the Side Deck zone. Side Deck Inform the other party exactly of the purchase amount.
- **Check the starting card**
The starting card 5 is displayed so that the opponent can check it accurately before the game starts.

### Checklist before you start

- [ ] Characters, characteristics, and ultimates are revealed on the front.
- [ ] Initially Priority was determined by an agreed random method.
- [ ] Notified the opponent of the number of decks purchased.
- [ ] The cards excluding list 9 and starting hand 5 were placed in Side Deck.
- [ ] Side Deck The purchase was notified to the other party.
- [ ] We confirmed the starting card 5.

# 4. Win/loss decision and Failure to Respond



## 4.1 Loss conditions specified in floor rules

If any of the following happens, that player loses:

- When one player's HP reaches 0
- When one player accumulates `Failure to Respond` 3 times

At the same time, refer to [7. Competition time limit and timeout processing](#floor-match-time) for handling when physical strength reaches 0 and when the time limit expires.

<a id="floor-no-response"></a>
## 4.2 Failure to Respond (not supported)

The following situation is treated as `What did not respond`, and `Failure to Respond` is accumulated 1 times.

- If the opponent does not ready the Technique within 10 seconds after it is ready
- If you do not have the Techniques to prepare
- When a Technique that does not meet the conditions for use is ready

### Failure to Respond Processing

1. The opposing player checks the hand of the player who did not respond.
2. The opponent selects the desired card 1 from his hand and makes it ready.
3. If you have no ready Techniques, it is treated as if you used Defense Technique, which has no defense roll, or Attack Technique, which has a slow speed.
4. In this case, the opposing player can apply either `hit` or `counter` to his Attack Technique.

> [! WARNING]
> The original text specifies “Defense Technique with no defense judgment or Attack Technique with slow speed,” but does not provide the exact card type, location, and speed values of the replacement technique. Web calculators or automatic adjudicators should not generate arbitrary numbers.

## 4.3 Deliberate Failure to Respond

If an opponent suspects that a player has intentionally chosen not to respond in order to gain a play advantage, such as by provoking an opponent or avoiding a Technique usage penalty, the opponent may ask the judge to make a decision.

If you determine that the judge is malicious, you can apply the following together:

- Accumulated `Failure to Respond` 1 times
- An additional penalty of not less than `warning` for sportsmanship violations.

<a id="floor-phase-operation"></a>
# 5. Progress and time limit for each phase



<a id="floor-ready-phase"></a>
## 5.1 Ready Phase

- When one player readies a Technique, the other player must do so within 10 seconds.
- If necessary, you can count time using a reliable means such as a fob, smartphone, or hourglass.

<a id="floor-ready-delay"></a>
## 5.2 Ready time delay on both sides

If neither player has their Technique ready for more than 30 seconds, Judge may impose a 30 second timeout.

| step | processing |
| --- | --- |
| No one is ready even within the first 30 second time limit. | Charges `caution` 1 times to both players, then grants 30 seconds again. |
| No one is ready even within the second 30 second time limit. | Game ends with both sides losing |

<a id="floor-multiple-ready"></a>
## 5.3 When multiple cards are ready at the same time

Even though it is not a unique effect designated by the game, if more than 2 cards are ready at the same time by hiding one card from view, etc., it is processed as follows.

- The player will be charged `Failure to Respond` 1 times
- Treated as not ready
- game resume

<a id="floor-get-phase"></a>
## 5.4 Get Phase

- You must decide which Technique to bring to your hand within 30 seconds.
- After 30 seconds, it is assumed that no cards were taken and the player's actions are terminated.
- The cards you bring are shown to your opponent so that they can be clearly seen.
- If you bring a Technique when the number of cards has reached the upper limit, immediately place the number of cards on the list equal to the number of cards exceeded.

<a id="floor-lumen-recovery"></a>
## 5.5 Lumens·Recovery Phase

- If there is an effect to deal with, when entering the phase, notify the opponent that there is an effect to deal with.
- Effect processing must be declared within 30 seconds after notification.
- After 30 seconds, the effect is considered unprocessed.

### If you haven't processed the forcing effect

- Opposing players can ask the judge.
- Judges may charge `caution` or `warning` depending on their impact on the game.

### If a phase passes without notice

If the effect was not notified, or the next phase action was quickly performed before notification and the phase was skipped, the judge imposes `caution` on the at-fault player and has the effect processed again.

# 6. Record and game state discrepancies



<a id="floor-notes"></a>
## 6.1 handwriting

- It is prohibited to separately record techniques brought into the hand or techniques used in Ready Phase.
- Recording for HP and FP verification is permitted.
- Use of the application for fitness and FP display purposes is also permitted.

<a id="floor-hp-discrepancy"></a>
## 6.2 When the physical strength records of both sides are different

You can pause the game and ask the judge. The original text gives an example of a situation where a card is revealed to Ready Phase and then the record difference is checked just before the effect is processed.

### When records are available

1. Under the supervision of the judge, check the physical strength recorded just before the situation.
2. Each player's HP is determined by the lower of both records.
3. Resume the game with that value.
4. Both players are charged `warning` 1 times.

> [! EXAMPLE]
> If one record is `A 3700 / B 4300` and the other is `A 3900 / B 4200`, the game is restarted with `A 3700 / B 4200` and both sides receive 1 warnings.

### If your records cannot be fully verified

- Adjust both players' HP to 2500 and resume the game.
- Both players are charged `warning` 1 times.

<a id="floor-hand-limit"></a>
## 6.3 When the hand limit is exceeded

- If you exceed the hand limit, immediately discard the number of cards in your hand equal to the number of cards exceeded. The cards discarded in Lumen Condenser are placed on the list.
- If a condition that is not observed is discovered during the game, `warning` 1 times will be charged and the excess will be discarded immediately.
- If the referee or tournament organizer determines that the severity is severe, `Game Loss` may be imposed for the game.

> [! WARNING]
> This floor rule expresses the “immediate” excess as discard. If a separate comprehensive rulebook further refines when to check status, you should check for the most recent official ruling or stop ruling rather than arbitrarily prioritizing the two documents.

# 7. Competition time limit and timeout processing



<a id="floor-default-match-time"></a>
## 7.1 default game time

If the competition does not specify a separate time, the following applies.

| Category | How to play | time limit |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| swiss round | single match | 30 minutes per round |
| finals | single match | 30 minutes per round |
| 3·4Weight | single match | 30 minutes per round |
| final | 3Pan 2Seonseung | 25 minutes per game |

<a id="floor-time-expiration"></a>
## 7.2 When the time limit is over but the game is not over

1. If there is an effect currently being processed, that effect will also be processed.
2. Compare your current physical strength.
3. The player with more HP wins.
4. If the HP is equal, the player with the current Priority wins.

<a id="floor-simultaneous-zero"></a>
## 7.3 When both HP points reach 0 at the same time

- Sudden Death Win or lose is determined by the game.
- The player who ultimately wins Sudden Death is the winner of the game.
- If Sudden Death is also a draw, the game is treated as a loss for both sides.

<a id="floor-sudden-death"></a>
## 7.4 Sudden Death

Floor rules stipulate that a new game begins with the following conditions:

- Both HP 1000
- Maximum number of hands
- Time limit 3 turns

> [! WARNING]
> The text of the floor rules only provides “maximum number of hands” and “3 turn” and does not explain detailed preparation procedures such as hand list, Side Deck reorganization or Priority maintenance. For detailed game procedures, check with [Sudden Death in the Comprehensive Rulebook](./lumen-comprehensive-rules.md#rule-12-2-1) and the latest official rulings.

<a id="floor-ranking-double-loss"></a>
## 7.5 When both sides lose in the final or ranking match

If both sides lose in a ranking match, such as the finals or the 3·4 place match, the winner will be determined in the following order.

1. In the match against Switzerland, the player with the higher number of wins and points wins.
2. If the competition record is the same, the ranking is determined by the agreement of both players using methods such as rock-paper-scissors, coin toss, or transfer.

# 8. Report results, surrender, abstain, be late



<a id="floor-result-report"></a>
## 8.1 Match result report

- The method of recording, reporting, and submitting results follows the method established for each competition.
- If signatures or verification are required from both players via a mobile device or on a win/loss result report, once submitted, the results cannot be overturned.

<a id="floor-match-fixing"></a>
## 8.2 Illegal match-fixing (abuse)

- If it is discovered that the players have intentionally manipulated their wins or losses, they will be disqualified from the competition regardless of their record.
- If match-fixing is discovered after the end of the competition, the following measures may be taken at the discretion of the competition organizer.
  - Match results and ranking corrections
  - Request for return of product
  - Deprivation of rights, including permanent suspension from participation in the relevant competition

<a id="floor-concession"></a>
## 8.3 surrender

If you admit defeat during a game, you will be treated as having lost the game.

<a id="floor-drop"></a>
## 8.4 Abstain

- Any player who wishes to withdraw during the competition may notify the judge or competition organizer and withdraw from the competition.
- If you withdraw after your opponent is announced, you will be treated as having lost the game.

<a id="floor-tardiness"></a>
## 8.5 late

After the opponent is announced, you must prepare for the game, but if no player shows up at the table, you will be considered late.

| tardy time | processing |
| --- | --- |
| Within 5 minutes after the round start declaration | `warning` |
| More than 5 minutes after round start declaration | lose the round |

- If you accumulate 3 warnings, you will be disqualified regardless of your competition record.
- Leaving your seat or affecting other matches without permission from the competition organizer may result in loss or disqualification.

<a id="floor-extra-time"></a>
## 8.6 Time Extension

Additional time may be given to the table in the following situations:

- If the player leaves the venue after the judge or competition organizer acknowledges the issue.
- When a player judges or challenges the judge and it is determined that the time taken to make the decision affected the game.

Additional time is the time spent on the matter plus 1 minutes.

<a id="floor-rulings-and-information"></a>
# 9. Rule judgment and information confirmation



<a id="floor-ruling-call"></a>
## 9.1 rule judgment

- During a competition, the game is basically played with both players checking the rules and effects with each other.
- If players find it difficult to judge among themselves or a conflict arises due to a difference of opinion, they must immediately stop the game and consult the judge.
- If play continues without a jersey check, both players are responsible for the consequences.
- People not playing the game, such as spectators, cannot inquire about the rules or intervene.
- If a judge discovers cheating or incorrect handling of rules or effects, he or she may intervene, request corrections, and impose a penalty without further inquiry.

<a id="floor-opponent-card-check"></a>
## 9.2 Check your opponent's card

Except in the following cases, you cannot ask your opponent to check the cards in his hand.

- Card taken from Get Phase
- Card brought into hand by effect
- Card effect to see opponent's hand
- If the `Failure to Respond` declaration according to the Terms of Use is confirmed under Jersey supervision.

If you are forced to reveal your hand, you can ask the judge.

<a id="floor-cost-refusal"></a>
## 9.3 When Failure to Respond is declared due to refusal of usage conditions/cost payment

- If you refuse to pay the usage cost and declare Failure to Respond, the opposing player may check that player's hand under the judge's supervision.
- As a result of the confirmation, if the Technique's usage conditions and cost are met, the Technique must be used.
- Players attempting to call Failure to Respond may be subject to a `warning` or more severe penalty based on the referee's decision.

<a id="floor-mobile-device"></a>
## 9.4 Mobile device precautions

If the judge or competition organizer determines that you influenced the outcome of the game by taking notes or using a mobile phone or tablet without showing the screen to your opponent, you may be penalized.

Use of applications to display HP and FP is permitted, but any other recording or manipulation will be judged in accordance with the above rules.

<a id="floor-penalties"></a>
# 10. penalty



> [! IMPORTANT]
> The example below is a representative example presented in the original text. The actual penalty is applied based on the judgment of the offender or competition organizer regarding the intentionality of the violation, impact on the game, repeatability, and disruption to competition operations. This web page is not an automatic penalty determination tool.

## Penalty Summary

| penalty | Purpose of application | Accumulation/Results |
| --- | --- | --- |
| caution | A violation that can be immediately reverted to the correct state and does not affect the win or loss or give a particular player an advantage. | Warning When 3 times are accumulated, warning 1 times |
| warning | A repeat of a violation that may affect the outcome of a win or loss but is reversible, or a violation that warrants a caution. | Warning: If you accumulate 3 times, you will be disqualified from the competition/event. |
| Game Loss | If it is deemed unfair to recognize the game result due to a serious violation | If the game is lost, a more severe penalty may be imposed depending on the severity. |
| disqualification | Violations that cause loss of trust in the game or cause significant disruption to the operation of the competition | Exclusion of awards/products and modification of rankings possible |
| Suspension | Serious behavior that requires refusal or suspension from participation in official competitions or events | Refusal or suspension of participation after official notice |

<a id="floor-caution"></a>
## 10.1 caution

It can be immediately reverted to correct play without affecting the outcome of the game, or it can be imposed on violations that do not give either side a particular advantage.

If 3 warnings are accumulated, 1 warnings will be issued.

### Original text example

- Accidentally showing Side Deck card 1 face up
- Accidentally revealed 1 card
- The opponent is not informed exactly what card the effect is triggered from.
- After Break, the process of replenishing the list from Side Deck is performed in the next phase.

<a id="floor-warning"></a>
## 10.2 warning

Although it may affect the outcome of the game, it is imposed when it is possible to return to correct play, or when caution-equivalent rules and manner violations are repeated while cautions have accumulated.

If you accumulate 3 warnings, you will be disqualified from the competition or event.

### Original text example

- Intentionally plays slowly so that the game does not end within the time limit
- Force effects are intentionally ignored and not applied
- Technique effects are not handled correctly and are intentionally handled differently
- Using techniques that cannot be used due to conditions

<a id="floor-game-loss"></a>
## 10.3 Game Loss

A penalty may be imposed if the referee or competition organizer determines that a serious violation has such a significant impact on the outcome of the game that it would be unfair to recognize the result of the game.

Depending on the severity of the violation, more severe penalties may be imposed.

### Original text example

- Manipulating game content or results in consultation with an opponent
- Submitted deck list and actual deck contents are different
- Cards with errata applied are used as pre-errata effects.
- Repeatedly disobeying decision-making or violating rules to create a situation that is advantageous to oneself.

> [! NOTE]
> The same original text also stipulates that if intentional match-fixing (abuse) is confirmed in a separate section, a player will be disqualified from the competition. Do not automatically conflate the two provisions into one result, but rather leave specific matters to the judge or competition organizer to decide.

<a id="floor-disqualification"></a>
## 10.4 disqualification

A penalty may be imposed if a serious violation causes the player to lose confidence in the game or causes significant disruption to the operation of the competition.

If you are disqualified, you may be excluded from receiving awards and prizes and your ranking may be modified even after the end of the competition.

### Original text example

- Play with a distinguishable mark on the card or sleeve.
- Repetitive and malicious slow play to the extent that it greatly affects the outcome of the win or loss.
- Swap your hand with Side Deck to deceive your opponent.
- Cards that are not in the deck list are secretly added to the list or hand and used.
- Using a trick, replacing an already ready card with another card.
- Forcing match-fixing through threats, etc.

<a id="floor-suspension"></a>
## 10.5 Suspension

Players who are subject to Suspension will be refused or suspended from participating in officially hosted competitions and events through official notice.

### Original text example

- Acts that cause harm to other participants
- Attempting or committing an offense
- An act that violates public morals
